Connect to Telegram

SpiDrive needs an app ID from Telegram. It's free and takes a minute.

  1. Open my.telegram.org/apps and log in.
  2. Create an app (any name, for example SpiDrive).
  3. Copy App api_id and App api_hash here.

Log in to Telegram

Telegram will send a login code to your Telegram app.
