SpiDrive uses these free, open-source programs:

rclone        https://rclone.org          MIT License
              Connects SpiDrive's drive server to the drive letter.

WinFsp        https://winfsp.dev          GPLv3 with FLOSS exception
              Lets programs create drives in Windows. Installed separately
              by the SpiDrive installer when it is missing.
